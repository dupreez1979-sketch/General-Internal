Connect monday.com

Claude Navigator reads your workspaces and boards with a personal API token. It is stored encrypted in your Mac's Keychain and only ever sent to monday.com.

  1. In monday.com, click your profile picture, then Developers.
  2. Choose My access tokens, then Show and Copy.
  3. Paste it here.
